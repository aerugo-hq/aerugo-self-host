# Customer support FAQ: order history

Customers see their last 24 months of orders on their account page, in the app and on the website [^faq]. Older orders are not shown there; support can send a customer a statement of older orders on request, within three working days.

A customer who can't find a recent order should check that they signed in with the email address they ordered with. Orders placed as a guest appear only after the customer links them to their account from the order confirmation email.
